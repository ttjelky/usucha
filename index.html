The Ritual · 04 steps

From Leaf To Cup
In Four Minutes

Every cup follows the same slow sequence. It takes a little longer. You can taste why.

  1. 01

    Sift

    Two grams of ceremonial powder through a fine sieve — no clumps, no bitterness.

  2. 02

    Whisk

    80°C water, a bamboo chasen and a quick “M” motion until a fine, glossy crema forms.

  3. 03

    Pour

    Over cold-pressed oat or coconut milk and slow-frozen ice, layered so you see every shade.

  4. 04

    Pause

    Stir, sip, put the phone down. That's the whole point.
